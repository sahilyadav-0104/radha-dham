Introduction / Parichay

Radha Dham ("we", "app", "website") ek bhakti-based (devotional) app aur website hai — Shri Radha Rani ke bhajan, leela, darshan, japa counter, festival calendar, Braj Yatra guide, quiz aur status/reels ke liye. Ye page batata hai ki hum aapka data kaise handle karte hain. Chhote shabdon me: hum aapki koi personal information collect nahi karte.

1. Information We Collect

Hum aapse naam, email, phone number, ya koi personal identity nahi maangte. App chalane ke liye kisi login ya account ki zaroorat nahi hai.

  • Aapke device par (localStorage): aapki pasand ki theme (rang), bhasha, japa counter ki ginti, pasand ki reels, puzzle ka best score, aur "Mera Mandir" setup — ye sab sirf aapke apne phone/browser me save hota hai. Hum tak nahi aata.
  • Comments (Anubhav): agar aap khud se koi comment likh kar bhejte hain, to wo public roop se site par sabko dikhta hai. Kripya usme apni private jaankari (phone, address) na likhein.
  • Basic analytics: hum ek privacy-friendly analytics (Vercel Analytics) use karte hain jo sirf ye batata hai ki kitne log kaun sa page dekhte hain — isme aapki pehchaan nahi hoti, na hi cross-site tracking hota hai.

2. Third-Party Content / Embeds

App me kuch content doosri jagah se dikhaya jaata hai:

  • YouTube & Instagram: Status/Reels section me videos in platforms se embed hoti hain. Inhe dekhne par un platforms ki apni privacy policy lagti hai.
  • Bhajan audio & images: free/licensed source se.

Hum in third-party services ke data collection ke liye zimmedar nahi hain — kripya unki policy dekhein: YouTube/Google, Instagram/Meta.

3. How We Use Data

Jo thodi si jaankari hai (aapke device ki settings aur anonymous analytics) uska istemal sirf app ko behtar chalane aur aapka experience yaad rakhne ke liye hota hai. Hum data bechte nahi, na kisi ko dete hain.

4. Children's Privacy

Ye ek family-friendly devotional app hai. Hum jaan-boojh kar bacchon se koi personal data collect nahi karte.

5. Data Security

Kyunki hum personal data collect hi nahi karte, aapki private jaankari leak hone ka khatra nahi hai. App HTTPS (secure) connection par chalti hai.

6. Your Choices

  • Apne browser/app ka data (settings, counter) aap kabhi bhi apne phone ki settings se clear kar sakte hain.
  • Comment hata-na chahte hain to hum se sampark karein.

7. Changes to This Policy

Zaroorat padne par hum is policy ko update kar sakte hain. Naya version isi page par "Last updated" date ke saath dikhega.
