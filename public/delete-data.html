Hamare paas aapka kya data hai?

Radha Dham me koi account nahi banta — na login, na email, na phone number. Hamare server par aapka data sirf tab aata hai jab aap khud apni marzi se Contact page par apna anubhav bhejte hain. Us waqt ye save hota hai:

  • Naam — jo aapne us form me likha (asli naam hona zaroori nahi)
  • Anubhav ka text — jo aapne likha, ye site par sabko public dikhta hai
  • Ek anonymous device id — aapke browser me bani random id, jisse aapki pehchaan nahi hoti. Ye sirf isliye hai taaki galat content bhejne wale device ko rok saken.

Baaki sab cheezein — theme, bhasha, japa counter ki ginti, puzzle ka score, "Mera Mandir" ka setup — sirf aapke apne phone me rehti hain, hum tak aati hi nahi.

Kya delete hoga: aapka anubhav ka text, usme diya gaya naam, aur uske saath judi anonymous device id — teeno hamesha ke liye hat jayenge.
Kya rakha jayega: kuch bhi nahi. Delete hone ke baad hamare paas aapse juda koi record nahi bachta — na backup, na koi extra retention period.
Kitne din me: aam taur par 7 din ke andar.

Sirf ek cheez alag hai — Vercel Analytics ka anonymous page-count (jaise "aaj 40 log aaye"). Usme aapka naam, device id ya koi pehchaan hoti hi nahi, isliye use kisi ek vyakti se jod kar hataya nahi ja sakta.

Apne phone ka data khud mitana ho

Theme, bhasha, japa ginti, puzzle score aur "Mera Mandir" ka setup aapke apne browser me save hota hai. Use aap khud kabhi bhi mita sakte hain — browser ki settings me jaakar site data / storage clear kar dein, ya app ko phone ki settings se Clear data kar dein. Iske liye hume batane ki zaroorat nahi.
